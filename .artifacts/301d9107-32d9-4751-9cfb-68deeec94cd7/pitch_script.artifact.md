# 🎤 GramKavach Hackathon Pitch & Demo Script

This script is designed for a **3-5 minute demo**. It focuses on the problem, your unique solution (AI + Localization), and the impact.

---

## 1. The Hook (The "Why") - 30 Seconds
**Start with a real-world scenario:**
> "Imagine a farmer in a small village receiving a 'Collect Request' on his phone. He thinks he's receiving a government subsidy, but instead, he's about to lose his life's savings. This is the reality for millions of new digital users in India."
>
> "Namaste/Hello everyone, we are presenting **GramKavach** – a local-first digital safety shield designed specifically for the next billion users."

---

## 2. The Solution - 1 Minute
**Show the Dashboard:**
> "GramKavach doesn't just block fraud; it **explains** it. Our app monitors device signals like unknown calls, remote-access apps, and suspicious links in real-time."
>
> **Key Points to mention:**
> - **Privacy First**: We don't read messages or intercept PINs. We observe the *context*.
> - **Explainable AI**: We don't just give a score; we give a breakdown of *why* a payment is risky.

---

## 3. The "Power Move": Deep Localization - 1 Minute
**Switch language to Hindi or Gujarati during the demo:**
> "But here is our most powerful feature: **Language is not a barrier to safety.**"
>
> *(Switch language live)*
>
> "GramKavach is 100% localized. Not just the buttons, but the entire AI logic, the transaction history, and the voice warnings work in **7 Indian languages** including Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, and Bengali."
>
> *(Play a Voice Warning in a regional language)*

---

## 4. The Live Demo (4 Scenarios) - 1.5 Minutes
**Click the simulation buttons one by one:**

1.  **Level 1: Safe**: "Show the green 'System Protected' state for verified contacts."
2.  **Level 2: Phishing**: "Demonstrate how we catch suspicious links from SMS."
3.  **Level 3: UPI Collect**: "Explain that receiving money *never* requires a PIN."
4.  **Level 4: Bank Scam**: "Show the critical alert when an unknown caller asks the user to open a remote-access app."

---

## 5. The Closing (The Impact) - 30 Seconds
> "In case of an actual fraud, we provide a direct shortcut to the **1930 Cyber Cell**. GramKavach is more than an app; it's a digital bodyguard for rural India."
>
> "Built with Kotlin, Bhashini AI, and a heart for a safer digital Bharat. Thank you!"

---

## 💡 Pro-Tips for the Demo:
- **Don't stay on one screen**: Judges love to see the UI react (animations, gauge pulsing, language switching).
- **Emphasize Bhashini**: Mention you are using Bhashini for voice alerts – it's a huge plus for "Made in India" initiatives.
- **Mention ONNX**: Briefly say the AI scoring is ready for edge-ML using ONNX for low latency.

🏆 **Good luck! You have a winning product in your hands.** 🛡️✨
