### *AI-Powered Pre-PIN Fraud Prevention for Rural India*

## 🚀 1. Executive Summary
GramKavach is a **Proactive Digital Bodyguard** designed to protect India's "Next Billion Users" from digital financial fraud. Unlike traditional reactive security systems, GramKavach identifies risks **before money leaves the account**. By monitoring on-device signals and providing regional voice alerts, it empowers rural users to use UPI safely and confidently.

## 🎯 2. The "Real India" Problem
Rural India has seen a massive surge in UPI adoption, but low digital literacy has made these users prime targets for:
- **Fake SMS/WhatsApp Links**: Phishing attempts to steal credentials.
- **Screen-Sharing Scams**: Use of apps like AnyDesk/TeamViewer to hijack devices.
- **UPI Collect Traps**: "Paisa lene ke liye PIN dalo" (Pay-to-Receive scams).

Existing solutions are largely English-centric and alert users *after* a loss. GramKavach fills this gap with **Pre-PIN Prevention**.

## 💡 3. The Innovation: Cyber-Kavach
GramKavach operates on three innovative layers:
1.  **"Zero-Trust" Signal Bus**: A unified architecture that continuously monitors unknown calls, remote-access apps, and suspicious URLs in real-time.
2.  **Hybrid AI Scorer**: Combines local pattern-matching with an **ONNX Mobile ML Engine** to calculate risk scores (0-100) instantly without any server latency.
3.  **Bhashini Voice Assistant**: Uses AI-powered text-to-speech to warn users in their native language (**Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, etc.**), breaking the literacy barrier.

## 🏆 4. Maverick Effect Alignment
- **Mindful Innovation**: Solves a critical financial safety problem with a unique "Pre-PIN" logic.
- **Frugal & Scalable**: Operates **100% Offline** with zero server costs, making it accessible to users without data plans.
- **Societal Impact**: Builds digital trust in Bharat's economy by protecting vulnerable first-time users.
